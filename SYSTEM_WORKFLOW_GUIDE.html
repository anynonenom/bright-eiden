Introduction
What is SCC?

SCC (Social Content Center) is EIDEN Group's internal platform for managing, scheduling, and publishing social media content across all platforms from one place.

💡

One platform, all channels

Instead of logging into Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok separately — everything happens inside SCC. You write the post once, choose which platforms to publish to, and the system handles the rest.

📝

Write posts, attach images/videos, preview how they look on each platform before publishing.

Workspace → Create

Workspace → Calendar

Structure
How the System is Organized

SCC uses a two-level structure: Organization at the top, Workspaces underneath.

Level
What it is
Organization
Your company (EIDEN Group). Contains all members, billing, and settings. One per company.
Workspace
A project or brand (e.g. "EIDEN Instagram", "Client ABC"). Each workspace has its own connected social accounts, calendar, and team.
Member
A team member belongs to the Organization and can be assigned to one or more Workspaces with a specific role.
ℹ️

Think of it like this

The Organization is the office building. Each Workspace is a room inside. Members can have keys to multiple rooms, with different permissions in each.

Permissions
Roles and What They Can Do

Each member has an Organization role and can also have a Workspace role.

👑

Owner

  • Full access to everything
  • Manage billing and org settings
  • Add and remove admins
  • Cannot be removed
🛡️

Admin

  • Invite and remove members
  • Create and manage workspaces
  • Configure social accounts
  • Approve or reject posts
✏️

Member

  • Create and schedule posts
  • Upload media
  • Submit posts for approval
  • View calendar and inbox
👁️

Viewer (Workspace)

  • Read-only access
  • See calendar and posts
  • Cannot create or edit
  • Used for client access
Daily Workflow
How a Post Goes from Idea to Published

Follow these steps every time you need to create and publish content.

1

Select your Workspace

From the left sidebar, click the workspace you are working in (e.g. "EIDEN Instagram"). All work happens inside a workspace.

Sidebar → Workspace Name
2

Capture the Idea (Optional)

If the content isn't ready to post yet, save it as an Idea first. The Idea Board is a Kanban board where you can organize content by status (e.g. "Idea", "In Progress", "Ready").

Create → Ideas Board Optional step
3

Write the Post in the Composer

Click Create → New Post. Write your caption, upload media, and choose which social accounts to publish to. You can write platform-specific variations of the same post.

Create → New Post
💡 Use the live preview panel to see exactly how your post will look on each platform before saving.
4

Upload Media (if needed)

Images and videos can be uploaded directly in the Composer, or picked from the Media Library. The library keeps all assets organized so you can reuse them across posts.

Settings → Media Library Or upload directly in Composer
5

Schedule or Submit for Approval

Choose a publish date and time, then either:

• Schedule directly — if you have permission to publish without approval.
• Submit for approval — if your workspace requires a manager to approve first.

Save as Draft → Schedule → Submit for Approval
6

Approval Review (Admins)

If the post requires approval, the admin will see it in the Approvals queue. They can approve, request changes with a comment, or reject the post.

Workspace → Approvals
📨 The author is notified when their post is approved, rejected, or has comments.
7

Post Goes Live Automatically

At the scheduled time, the system publishes the post to all selected platforms automatically. No action needed — the background worker handles it.

✓ Fully automatic
8

Monitor the Calendar and Inbox

After publishing, check the Calendar to see all past and upcoming posts. Check the Inbox to reply to comments and messages received from any connected account.

Workspace → Calendar Workspace → Inbox

Post Status Flow

Every post moves through these statuses on its way to being published.

📝 Draft
→
🔍 In Review
→
✅ Approved
→
📅 Scheduled
→
🚀 Published
Status
Meaning
Draft
Post is saved but not submitted. Only the author can see it. Still editable.
In Review
Post has been submitted for approval. Waiting for admin/manager to review. Author cannot edit.
Changes Requested
Admin reviewed and left comments. Author must revise and resubmit.
Approved
Admin approved the post. It can now be scheduled or published immediately.
Scheduled
Post has a publish date set. The system will publish it automatically at that time.
Published
Post has gone live on the selected platforms. Visible in the calendar as a published item.
Failed
The system tried to publish but encountered an error (e.g. expired token). Reconnect the social account and retry.
Team Setup
How to Add a New Team Member

Only Admins and Owners can invite new members. Follow these steps.

1

Go to Organization Members

From the sidebar, click Settings → Members. This is at the organization level, not workspace level.

Settings → Members
2

Click "Invite Member"

Enter the person's email address, choose their organization role (Admin or Member), and optionally assign them to one or more workspaces with a specific workspace role.

3

Share the Invitation Link

After sending the invite, use the Copy Link button next to the invitation and send it directly to the person (via WhatsApp, email, or any other channel). This is the most reliable method.

⚠️ Email delivery may sometimes be delayed. The Copy Link button is always available as a direct alternative.
4

New Member Accepts the Invite

When they open the link, they will be asked to create an account (or log in if they already have one). Once accepted, they appear in the Members list and can access their assigned workspaces immediately.

Client Portal
Giving Clients Access

Clients can be invited to review and approve content without seeing internal tools or team settings.

🏢

What clients can do

Clients see a simplified portal with the content scheduled for their workspace. They can approve or request changes on posts. They cannot create posts, invite members, or access any settings.

1

Open Client Portal Settings

Go to the workspace → Settings → Client Portal.

Workspace → Settings → Client Portal
2

Invite the Client by Email

Enter the client's email and send the invitation. Use Copy Link to send the invite URL directly to them.

3

Client Reviews Content

The client accesses their portal at scc.eiden-group.com/portal/. They can view all content prepared for them and give feedback directly.

⚠️

If something is not working

Most issues come from one of three things: (1) a social account token expired — reconnect it in Settings → Social Accounts; (2) a post failed to publish — check its status in the Calendar for an error message; (3) you don't have permission — ask your Admin to check your workspace role.
