# How to Connect Your Social Media Accounts to SCC

This guide is for team members and clients who need to connect their social media platforms to the EIDEN GROUP SCC platform so content can be published on their behalf.

---

## Before You Start

You will need:
- An invitation link sent to you by your workspace admin (check your email)
- Admin access to the social media accounts you want to connect
- A few minutes per platform

---

## Step 1 — Accept Your Invitation

1. Open the invitation email sent to you
2. Click the **"Accept Invitation"** button
3. Create your account or log in if you already have one
4. You will land on your workspace dashboard

---

## Step 2 — Go to Social Accounts

1. In the left sidebar, click **"Channels"** or go to:
   `Settings → Social Accounts`
2. Click **"Connect Account"**
3. Choose the platform you want to connect

---

## Platform-by-Platform Instructions

### Facebook (Pages)

**Requirements:** You must be an Admin of the Facebook Page you want to connect.

1. Click **Connect → Facebook**
2. A Facebook login window will open — log in with the account that manages your Page
3. Select **"Opt in to all current and future Pages"**
4. Select your **Business** (if prompted)
5. Click **Continue → Save**
6. Back in SCC, select which Page to connect from the list
7. Click **Confirm**

> **Note:** Only Facebook Pages can be connected — not personal profiles.

---

### Instagram (Business/Creator)

**Requirements:** Your Instagram account must be a **Business** or **Creator** account AND linked to a Facebook Page.

**To switch to a Business account on Instagram:**
Settings → Account → Switch to Professional Account → Business

**To link to a Facebook Page:**
Instagram Settings → Account → Linked Accounts → Facebook → connect your Page

Then in SCC:
1. Click **Connect → Instagram**
2. Log in with Facebook (the account linked to your Instagram)
3. Grant the requested permissions
4. Select your Instagram account from the list
5. Click **Confirm**

> **Note:** Personal Instagram accounts cannot be connected.

---

### LinkedIn (Personal Profile)

**Requirements:** A LinkedIn personal account.

1. Click **Connect → LinkedIn (Personal)**
2. A LinkedIn login window will open
3. Click **Allow** to grant posting permissions
4. You will be redirected back — connection complete

---

### LinkedIn (Company Page)

**Requirements:** You must be a **Super Admin** of the LinkedIn Company Page.

1. Click **Connect → LinkedIn (Company)**
2. Log in with your LinkedIn account
3. Select the Company Page from the list
4. Click **Allow**

---

### TikTok

**Requirements:** A TikTok account (personal or business).

1. Click **Connect → TikTok**
2. Log in with your TikTok credentials
3. Grant video publishing permissions
4. Connection complete

> **Note:** TikTok requires videos only — no image or text posts.

---

### YouTube

**Requirements:** A Google account with a YouTube channel.

1. Click **Connect → YouTube**
2. Log in with your Google account
3. Select the YouTube channel to connect
4. Grant the requested permissions
5. Connection complete

---

### Pinterest

**Requirements:** A Pinterest business account.

1. Click **Connect → Pinterest**
2. Log in with your Pinterest credentials
3. Grant board and pin management permissions
4. Connection complete

---

### Google Business Profile

**Requirements:** A Google account with access to a Business Profile (formerly Google My Business).

1. Click **Connect → Google Business**
2. Log in with your Google account
3. Select the Business Profile location
4. Grant permissions
5. Connection complete

---

### Bluesky

**Requirements:** A Bluesky account. You will use an **App Password** — NOT your main password.

**To create an App Password on Bluesky:**
1. Go to bsky.app → Settings → Privacy and Security → **App Passwords**
2. Click **Add App Password**
3. Give it a name (e.g. "SCC")
4. Copy the generated password

**Then in SCC:**
1. Click **Connect → Bluesky**
2. Enter your Bluesky handle (e.g. `yourname.bsky.social`)
3. Enter the **App Password** (not your main password)
4. Click **Connect**

---

### Mastodon

**Requirements:** A Mastodon account on any instance.

1. Click **Connect → Mastodon**
2. Enter your instance URL (e.g. `mastodon.social`)
3. You will be redirected to your instance to log in and authorize
4. Connection complete

---

### Threads

**Requirements:** A Threads account linked to Instagram.

1. Click **Connect → Threads**
2. Log in with your Instagram/Facebook credentials
3. Grant the requested permissions
4. Connection complete

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| "Invalid scope" error | Your account may not have Business/Creator status. Check platform requirements above. |
| Can't see my Page/Account in the list | Make sure you are an Admin of that Page/Account on the platform itself. |
| Connection keeps failing | Try disconnecting and reconnecting. Make sure your browser allows popups for this site. |
| Token expired / Reconnect button showing | Click **Reconnect** and go through the login flow again. Tokens expire periodically. |
| Instagram not showing up | Make sure your Instagram is linked to a Facebook Page and switched to Business/Creator mode. |
