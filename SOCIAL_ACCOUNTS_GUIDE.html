Overview
What is Social Account Connection?

Connecting a social account allows the platform to publish posts, schedule content, and manage your pages — all from one place.

ℹ️

Before you start

You need to be logged in to scc.eiden-group.com and have access to a Workspace. Social accounts are connected per workspace, not per user. Make sure you are inside the correct workspace before connecting.

⚠️

You need admin credentials for each platform

To connect Facebook, Instagram, or LinkedIn, you must be an Admin or Page Manager on that account. If you are not the page admin, ask the account owner to do this step.

Step by Step
How to Connect an Account

Follow these steps in order. The process takes about 2 minutes per platform.

1

Open your Workspace Settings

From the left sidebar, click on your workspace name, then navigate to Settings. Look for the Social Accounts section.

Workspace → Settings → Social Accounts
2

Click "Connect Account"

You will see a grid of available platforms. Click the platform you want to connect. A new window or redirect will open to that platform's login page.

💡 Each platform is listed with its icon. If a platform is grayed out, it means the admin has not yet configured app credentials for it — contact your manager.
3

Log in and Authorize

You will be taken to the platform's official login page (Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, etc.). Log in with the account you want to connect, then click Allow or Authorize when asked for permissions.

✅ Only grant access to the pages or accounts you intend to manage. You can always disconnect later.
4

Select the Page or Profile (Facebook / Instagram)

For Facebook and Instagram, after login you may be shown a list of Pages you manage. Select the specific Page you want to connect to this workspace. You can connect multiple pages.

5

Confirm the Connection

You will be redirected back to scc.eiden-group.com. The connected account will now appear in the Social Accounts list with a green checkmark. It is ready to use for scheduling and publishing.

Platforms
Supported Social Platforms

All platforms below are supported. Connection method varies by platform.

📘

Facebook

OAuth Login
  • Connects Pages you manage
  • Must be Page Admin or Editor
  • Supports scheduling posts & reels
📸

Instagram

OAuth Login
  • Requires Instagram Business or Creator account
  • Must be linked to a Facebook Page
  • Connect via Facebook login flow
💼

LinkedIn Personal

OAuth Login
  • Connects your personal profile
  • Log in with your LinkedIn credentials
  • Posts appear on your personal feed
🏢

LinkedIn Company

OAuth Login
  • Connects a Company Page
  • Must be Page Super Admin
  • Posts appear on the company page
🎵

TikTok

OAuth Login
  • Connect a TikTok Business account
  • Log in with TikTok credentials
  • Schedule and publish videos
▶️

YouTube

OAuth Login
  • Connect a YouTube Channel
  • Log in with Google account
  • Schedule and upload videos
📌

Pinterest

OAuth Login
  • Connect a Pinterest Business account
  • Log in with Pinterest credentials
  • Schedule pins to boards
🦋

Bluesky

Direct Login
  • Enter your Bluesky handle and App Password
  • Create an App Password in Bluesky Settings
  • Do NOT use your main password
🐘

Mastodon

Instance OAuth
  • Enter your Mastodon instance URL
  • e.g. mastodon.social or your own server
  • Authorize via your instance login page
Special Instructions
Bluesky — App Password Setup

Bluesky does not use the standard OAuth flow. You need to create an App Password first.

1

Go to Bluesky Settings

Open bsky.app → click your profile → Settings → Privacy and Security → App Passwords.

2

Create a New App Password

Click Add App Password, give it a name like "EIDEN SCC", and copy the generated password. It looks like: xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx

⚠️ You will only see this password once. Copy it immediately before closing the window.
3

Enter in SCC

In SCC → Social Accounts → Connect Bluesky. Enter your handle (e.g. yourname.bsky.social) and paste the App Password.

Troubleshooting
Common Issues
Problem
What to do
"Platform not available"
The admin has not configured API credentials for this platform yet. Contact your manager.
Redirect error after login
The platform rejected the callback URL. This is an admin issue — ask them to verify the OAuth redirect URI in the developer app settings.
No pages shown (Facebook)
You are not an Admin or Editor on any Facebook Page. Ask the Page owner to grant you the correct role first.
Account disconnects itself
The OAuth token expired or was revoked. Simply reconnect the account using the same steps above.
Instagram not showing
Instagram must be a Business or Creator account and linked to a Facebook Page. Personal Instagram accounts cannot be connected.
Bluesky login fails
Make sure you are using an App Password, not your main Bluesky password. Create a new App Password from bsky.app Settings.
Need Help?
Contact Your Admin
💬

Something not working?

If you have followed all steps and the connection still fails, contact your workspace admin. Most issues (platform not available, redirect errors) can only be resolved by someone with admin access to the platform's developer settings.
